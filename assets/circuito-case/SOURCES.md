# Circuito Mágico del Agua: case-study source

Source supplied by the portfolio owner: `Circuito_Magico_Portafolio_Mockups_Realistas.pdf`, 17 pages.

The page is an academic, collaborative proposal. Environmental images are explicitly presented as mockups, not evidence of actual installation or measured results. Safety graphics are shown as design artifacts; no certification/compliance claims have been added.

Source mapping:
- Pages 1–4: context, diagnosis, direction and academic nature.
- Page 5: moodboard and visual concept.
- Page 6: typography and exact four brand color values.
- Pages 7–8: original sketches and digital grids.
- Pages 9–10: pictograms, restrictions and safety graphics.
- Pages 11–12: totem family and map proposal.
- Pages 13–16: orientation, information, prohibition and warning mockups.
- Page 17: synthesis and team credits (Andrea Villarreal Ledesma, Valentino Levano Encinas, Fiorela Soto Cardenas).

Artwork is extracted from the original 2560 × 1440 raster pages. Crops remove slide text/margins or separate montage panels, without regenerating or redrawing artwork. WebP images preserve native crop dimensions; wide images additionally have 720px variants. The existing 28-page interactive manual is a separate, unchanged document.
